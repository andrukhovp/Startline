Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Startline is a free planner for starting an online business. It is run by ("we"). Contact:

Startline shows no ads, sells no data, and uses no advertising trackers or cookies.

Using Startline without an account

Everything you enter (your plan answers, tasks, notes, saved products, sites, sales you log, saves and settings) is stored only in your browser on your device. It is not sent to us. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes it, so use Settings › Export a backup to keep a copy.

Using Startline with an account

  • What we store: your email address; your password, which is stored scrambled (hashed) by the sign-in service, never in readable form; the name you choose to give; and the work you save (business names, plan answers, tasks and notes, saved products and sites, sales you log, saves and milestones).
  • Where: in a database run by our service provider Supabase, which processes it on our behalf. The database only lets each account read its own data.
  • On your devices: a copy of your account's data is kept on each device where you sign in, so Startline works offline. It is removed from that device when you sign out.
  • Technical data: when your device connects, Supabase processes technical details such as your IP address and the time of the request, to run and secure the service.
  • Why: to provide the account you asked for, and to keep it secure.
  • How long: until you delete it. Account › Delete my account removes your sign-in and everything saved in your account at once. Copies in the provider's backups disappear as those backups expire.

Continue with Google: if you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and basic profile with the sign-in service. We store your email address.

Email about your account (confirming your address, resetting your password) is sent only when you ask for it.

Hosting

The site's files are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts and all of Startline's own files are served from this site, not from outside services.

Visitor statistics: we count visits and a few steps people reach in the app (for example "plan built" or "first step done") with , which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you. Nothing you type into Startline is included.

Other sites and AI apps

Startline links to platforms such as Shopify, Etsy or YouTube; when you open one, that site's own privacy policy applies. Startline doesn't send your information to AI services: its AI tools write a prompt that you copy into your own AI app yourself.

Your rights

You can see and export your data (Settings › Export a backup), correct it in the app, and delete it (delete items, a business, everything, or your whole account). Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to processing, to ask us for a copy, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise a right, contact us at the address above.

Children

You must be 16 or older to create an account. Most selling platforms require you to be 18, or to use a parent's or guardian's account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
